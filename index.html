Selected research

2026 · Preprint · Under review at The Visual Computer

Entropy-Gated Online Self-Distillation for Visual Speech Recognition Based on Connectionist Temporal Classification

Mehrad Yaghoubi, Azam Bastanfard

Using frame-level uncertainty to guide knowledge transfer in lightweight lip-reading models, with negligible additional inference cost.

Read paperDOI
Research summary

Some lip movements are visually ambiguous. This work studies an entropy-gated teacher–student approach that limits self-distillation when the teacher is uncertain. It combines lightweight visual speech recognition with CTC training to address unreliable frame-level guidance.

2026 · arXiv preprint

Belief-Guided Decision Making with Uncertainty Gating in the Game of Go

Mehrad Yaghoubi, Azam Bastanfard, Abbas Jalilvand, Ashkan Rezaei

Exploring how uncertainty-aware belief models can guide decision making in Go with less reliance on search.

Read paper
Research summary

This project examines belief-guided decision making and uncertainty gating in Go. It connects an interest in efficient models with the question of when a learned prediction is reliable enough to guide a decision.

Research interests

My current work brings together three connected questions.

Visual speech recognition

How can a model recognize speech when lip movements are ambiguous or some video frames carry little information?

Efficient visual models

How can lightweight architectures and knowledge distillation improve recognition while keeping inference affordable?

Learning with uncertainty

When should a model trust its predictions, and when should uncertainty limit the knowledge it transfers?
