The reading order puts identity and interests, manuscripts, research questions, software, education and contact within easy reach. No website ensures admission or funding. Concrete evidence is more useful than invented metrics.

## Language and search

[Google's multilingual guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites) supports separate language URLs with fully localized visible content. Four static pages share one template and content module. Each has its own canonical URL and reciprocal English/Persian hreflang links. Language selection is explicit.

English and Persian names appear naturally. Schema alternate names include the username, initial form and Arabic-codepoint normalization; the misspelling in the ResearchGate URL is not treated as another identity. Computer vision, VSR, lip reading, knowledge distillation and efficient deep learning appear in relevant content without keyword stuffing.

Static HTML, schema, sitemap and robots support discovery. `llms.txt` is a convenience for tools that choose to use it, not a guarantee that AI systems will read or cite the site. Indexing and ranking remain search-engine decisions. Search Console verification is preserved.
